# Lacspace Licensing

Not everything Lacspace builds is free — and this document explains how each kind
of package is licensed. The single source of truth, always current, is the
**[Lacspace Licence Centre](https://lacspace.com/licenses)**.

> **This repository** contains our **openly published** packages. Everything here
> is under the **Lacspace Free Licence** (see [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE)).

## The tiers

| Tier | Badge | What it covers | Terms |
|------|-------|----------------|-------|
| **[Lacspace Free Licence](https://lacspace.com/licenses/lacspace-free-1.0)** | Free | The packages we publish openly on the public `@lacspace` npm scope (this repo). | MIT-equivalent freedoms — use, modify, redistribute and sell, personal or commercial, at no cost. Just keep the notice. |
| **[Lacspace Commercial Licence](https://lacspace.com/licenses/lacspace-commercial)** | Paid | Our advanced, paid packages. | Licensed per application or per organisation, with updates and support. Not redistributable. |
| **[Lacspace Client Licence](https://lacspace.com/licenses/lacspace-client)** | Client | Bespoke packages built for a specific client. | Governed by the signed engagement / SOW — scope, ownership and support per contract. |
| **[Lacspace Private (Proprietary) Licence](https://lacspace.com/licenses/lacspace-proprietary)** | Private | Internal, confidential packages that power Lacspace products. | All rights reserved. Not published, not for sale. |

Most Lacspace packages are commercial, client-specific or private, and are used
privately. The free packages in this repository are the part we give away.

## What the Free Licence means

When a package carries the **Lacspace Free Licence**, you can:

- ✅ Use it in personal **and** commercial projects, at no cost
- ✅ Modify, merge, redistribute, sublicense and sell copies
- ✅ Use it privately, with no obligation to publish

…as long as you keep the copyright and licence notice in substantial copies. It
grants no rights to the Lacspace name, logo or trademarks. In the freedoms it
grants it is equivalent to the MIT Licence; it is source-available and permissive,
but not (as of v1.0) an OSI-approved licence.

- Read it online: **[lacspace.com/licenses/lacspace-free-1.0](https://lacspace.com/licenses/lacspace-free-1.0)**
